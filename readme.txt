Steps and timelines in several designs, with icons, numbers and colors running from the first step to the last.

== Description ==

Write the steps once (a title, a text, an icon, a side text) and choose how they are drawn:

* **List** — cards one under another, linked by a line or arrows.
* **Timeline** — a line with a marker for each step (a number, a ring, a dot), a side text on one side and the card on the other; or the cards from side to side.
* **Zigzag** — cards from side to side with a large number and a curved line between the markers.
* **Wave** — circles side by side with half rings that make a wave; texts below and above in turn.
* **Progress** — steps in a row, a marker for each on one line (a number, a ring, a dot, the step's icon, or a bar), the icon above it and the texts under it.

Every design adapts to the width it has: on a narrow block (a phone, or a narrow column on a wide screen) it folds into one column. Sizes can be set for desktop, tablet and phone.

Colors: the first and the last step's colors; the steps between take the colors in between. A step can have a color of its own. Cards: border, radius, padding, margin and shadow of your own, for all cards or for one step. Icons: 100 line icons of the plugin, or any image of the media library.

Insert the steps with the shortcode `[wp_steps id="123"]` or the WP Steps element in WPBakery Page Builder.

No script on the page: the designs are CSS only, and each design's stylesheet loads only where it is used.
